# Research Paper Generation Prompt

Use the following prompt to generate a high-quality, academic research paper. Replace the bracketed information with your specific project details.

---

## The Prompt

**Role:** You are an expert academic researcher and writer specializing in [Insert Field, e.g., Computer Science / Biotechnology / Economics]. You have a track record of publishing in high-impact, peer-reviewed journals and are skilled at synthesizing complex data into clear, rigorous, and persuasive academic prose.

**Task:** Write a comprehensive research paper titled "[Insert Working Title]".

**Objective:** The goal of this paper is to [Insert Primary Goal, e.g., demonstrate the efficacy of X in reducing Y / analyze the correlation between A and B].

**Context & Key Data:**
- **Core Thesis:** [Insert main argument or hypothesis]
- **Key Findings/Data Points:** [List 3-5 primary results or data points that must be included]
- **Methodology:** [Briefly describe the approach used: e.g., Randomized Controlled Trial, Systematic Review, Qualitative Analysis]
- **Target Audience:** [e.g., Academic peers, Policy makers, Industry experts]

**Structural Requirements:**
Please follow the standard IMRaD structure (Introduction, Methods, Results, and Discussion):

1. **Abstract:** A concise summary (approx. 250 words) covering the problem, method, key results, and conclusion.
2. **Introduction:** 
   - Establish the context and importance of the problem.
   - Provide a brief literature review.
   - Clearly state the research gap and the specific objectives of this paper.
3. **Methods:** 
   - Detail the experimental design/framework.
   - Describe the participants/dataset.
   - Explain the analytical tools and statistical methods used.
4. **Results:** 
   - Present findings objectively.
   - Reference specific data/tables (use placeholders like [Table 1: X vs Y]).
   - Highlight significant vs. non-significant results.
5. **Discussion & Conclusion:** 
   - Interpret the results in the context of previous research.
   - Discuss limitations and suggest future work.
   - State a clear final conclusion based on the evidence.
6. **References:** Use [Insert Style, e.g., APA 7th / IEEE / MLA] format.

**Tone and Style Guidelines:**
- **Academic Rigor:** Use formal, objective, and precise language. Avoid superlatives (e.g., "amazing," "incredible") and colloquialisms.
- **Clarity:** Prioritize clarity over complexity. Use active voice where appropriate but maintain a formal distance.
- **Evidence-Based:** Every claim must be supported by a citation or a specific result from the data.
- **Cohesion:** Ensure smooth transitions between sections and paragraphs using logical signposting.

**Constraints:**
- Do not hallucinate data. If a specific value is not provided in the context, use [INSERT DATA] as a placeholder.
- Ensure all technical terms are used accurately within the context of [Field].
- Maintain a word count of approximately [Insert desired length, e.g., 3,000 - 5,000 words].

## Instructions for Use
1. Copy the text between the lines above.
2. Fill in the `[Bracketed]` sections with your specific research details.
3. Paste it into your LLM of choice (Claude, GPT-4, etc.).
4. Review the output and iterate on specific sections (e.g., "Expand the Discussion section to further address the limitation of X").
